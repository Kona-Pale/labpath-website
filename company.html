COMPANY OVERVIEW

LabPath (Pty) Ltd is a specialised pathology service provider offering comprehensive diagnostic solutions across South Africa. We serve public and private hospitals, laboratory networks, medical clinics, healthcare professionals and individual patients.



LabPath was established with a vision to bring modern infrastructure, technological advancement and specialist expertise into healthcare. Through accurate testing, dependable reporting and responsive professional support, we help strengthen patient care and laboratory performance.

Our Mission

To establish world-class laboratories equipped with modern technology and guided by international standards and sound laboratory processes. We are committed to professional service delivered with a smile and a human touch, so that every interaction reflects care, respect and excellence.

To become a benchmark for quality healthcare and a global leader in holistic well-being by making preventive, predictive and diagnostic healthcare more accessible and helping to build a healthier future for all.
